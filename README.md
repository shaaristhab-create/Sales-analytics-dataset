# 📊 Sales Analytics Dashboard

An interactive **Sales Analytics Dashboard** designed to provide a clear overview of sales performance, revenue trends, customer activity, product performance, and regional insights.

The dashboard helps businesses monitor key performance indicators (KPIs), identify sales trends, and analyze product and regional performance in a single view.

## 🚀 Dashboard Overview

The dashboard provides an interactive view of sales data with filters for:

- 📅 **Month**
- 🌍 **Region**
- 📦 **Item Category**

Users can apply filters to analyze sales performance across different time periods, regions, and product categories.

## 📈 Dashboard Insights

### Monthly Sales Trend
Tracks revenue performance across different months and helps identify fluctuations and overall sales trends.

### Sales by Region
Compares sales performance across regions such as:

- East
- West
- North
- South

### Profit by Region
Visualizes the contribution of each region to overall profit using a donut chart.

### Top 5 Customers by Revenue
Highlights the customers generating the highest revenue, making it easier to identify valuable customers.

### Sales vs Cost by Product
Compares product-level sales and costs to understand product profitability and performance.

### Sales by Item Category
Provides a breakdown of sales across product categories such as:

- Backpack
- Chair
- Charger
- Desk Lamp
- Notebook
- Pen Set
- USB Drive

### Quantity Sold by Product
Shows the number of units sold for each product, helping identify high-volume products.

### Orders Over Time
Tracks order volume across months to identify changes in customer purchasing activity.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Power BI** – Dashboard development and data visualization
- **Data Modeling** – Relationships and analytical data structure
- **DAX** – KPI calculations and measures
- **Data Visualization** – Interactive charts, cards, slicers, and tables

## 🎯 Key Objectives

The dashboard was created to:

- Monitor overall sales performance
- Track revenue and order trends
- Analyze regional performance
- Identify high-performing products and categories
- Understand customer contribution to revenue
- Compare sales and product costs
- Support data-driven business decisions
